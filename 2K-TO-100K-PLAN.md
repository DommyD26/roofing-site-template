## What $100,000 actually requires

At your big-job average of **$7,321 per check: 13.7 jobs at $20k+.**

You have closed **10 such jobs in 22 months — 0.45 per month.** Hitting
$100k in 90 days means **4.6 big jobs per month, a 10x increase**, and every
one of them has to also *complete and get paid* inside the window.

Then the payout terms bite. 100% at completion, +10 days after the check
clears, closeout in by Sunday midnight, paid the following Thursday — that's
**~11 weeks from signature to your account** on a standard insurance job,
**~6 weeks** on an already-approved claim or a cash job.

**Your best six months ever was $48,979 (H2 2025). Your best quarter is
roughly $25,000.**

## The strategy: stop selling repairs, start selling buildings

### Priority 1 — work your existing accounts before you dial a stranger

This is where 42% of your money already came from, it costs $0, and it has no
cold-call cycle. In week 1, before anything else:

- **Call Quikrete (Frazier), Langley (Webb/Dye/Chaloupka), Austin Career
  Institute (Stroh/Branham), Superior Pipeline (Weeks), and Funk.** Ask one
  question: *"What other properties do you have, and when were their roofs
  last looked at?"* Offer a free portfolio condition survey across every
  building they own.
- **3410 N PanAm Expressway has produced four jobs.** Find out who else owns
  buildings on that corridor and who manages them.
- Every one of these is commercial, already trusts you, and skips the entire
  trust-building phase that makes cold calls close at 10%.

### Priority 2 — take the whole job, not a split

Shared jobs were **59% of your volume and 34% of your pay**. A $30k solo job
pays you what a $60k shared job pays. On anything you source yourself, own it.

### Priority 3 — qualify OUT small work

New rule: **an inspection has to have $20k+ of potential or it doesn't get a
morning slot.** On the phone, before booking:

- Full replacement potential, or an insurance claim — not a single leak
- Roof 10+ years, owner-occupied, no claim in 5 years
- **"Do you own any other properties?"** — asked on every single call
- Commercial or multi-property owner → book it same day

Small repairs don't disappear — they go to a **Friday afternoon repair block**
or get handed off. They stay good for relationships and referrals; they just
stop eating your prime selling hours.

### Priority 4 — hunt already-approved claims

An approved claim with no contractor yet cuts signature-to-paid from ~11 weeks
to ~6. Ask *"have you already filed a claim or had an adjuster out?"* in the
first thirty seconds. Ask your data vendor for **storm-date lists 12–24 months
back** — those are full of approved claims that were never built.

### Priority 5 — compress the cycle

- **Mortgage company endorsement is the biggest hidden delay** (2–6 weeks).
  Find out at signing whether the loan requires it; have the homeowner start
  the packet the day the check is *issued*.
- **Never let a check clear Fri/Sat/Sun.** Clearing Mon–Thu pays in **17 days**;
  Fri–Sun pays in **24**. Time your deposits.
- **Submit the closeout the hour the check clears.** Missing Sunday midnight by
  an hour costs seven days. Recurring alarm, Sunday 8 PM.
